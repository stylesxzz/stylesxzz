<h2>🕶️ Grey Hat Security Research</h2>

<p>
My interest in Grey Hat security research comes from exploring the
intersection between offensive security, vulnerability analysis, and
defensive improvement. I study how applications and systems behave,
how weaknesses can emerge, and how security findings can contribute
to stronger and more resilient software.
</p>

<p>
My security interests include:
</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Vulnerability Research:</strong> Investigating potential weaknesses in web applications, authentication mechanisms, access controls, APIs, and application logic.</li>
<li><strong>Penetration Testing:</strong> Exploring offensive security methodologies in authorized environments, controlled laboratories, and programs that explicitly permit testing.</li>
<li><strong>Red Team:</strong> Studying adversarial techniques and attack scenarios to understand how systems can be compromised and how defenses can be improved.</li>
<li><strong>Blue Team:</strong> Exploring defensive security, threat analysis, security monitoring, incident investigation, and remediation strategies.</li>
<li><strong>Security Analysis:</strong> Assessing potential technical impact, documenting findings, and developing clear recommendations to reduce security risks.</li>
<li><strong>Responsible Disclosure:</strong> Communicating valid findings through appropriate channels and respecting program scope, authorization, and applicable laws.</li>
</ul>

<p>
I approach Grey Hat research with technical curiosity and a focus on
understanding systems beyond their visible interfaces. My goal is to
investigate security weaknesses, understand their real-world implications,
and contribute to more secure technology.
</p>

<p>
I also recognize the importance of responsible security research:
testing should remain within systems I own, environments for which I
have explicit authorization, or activities permitted by a program's
published scope.
</p>

<p>
<strong>My philosophy: understand the attack surface, identify the weakness,
document the evidence, and help improve the defense.</strong>
</p>
