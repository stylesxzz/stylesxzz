## 🕶️ Grey Hat Security Research

My interest in Grey Hat security research comes from the desire to understand systems beyond their visible interfaces, investigate potential vulnerabilities, and learn how real-world security weaknesses emerge.

This area of interest includes:

- **Vulnerability discovery:** examining application behavior, authentication flows, access controls, input validation, and other potential attack surfaces.
- **Security analysis:** studying how weaknesses can affect confidentiality, integrity, availability, and user trust.
- **Offensive security:** learning and applying penetration-testing methodologies in authorized environments, laboratories, CTFs, and approved security programs.
- **Defensive improvement:** translating findings into practical remediation recommendations and more secure development practices.
- **Responsible disclosure:** documenting findings clearly and communicating them through the appropriate reporting channels.

I value the technical curiosity associated with Grey Hat research while recognizing the importance of legal boundaries and responsible conduct. Security testing should be limited to systems I own or have explicit permission to assess, or activities expressly permitted by a program's published scope.

**My objective is not simply to find weaknesses, but to understand their impact and help make technology more resilient.**
